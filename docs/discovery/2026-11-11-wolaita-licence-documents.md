## What the documents settle

1. **Driving schools are licensed by the region, on the zone's recommendation** (Q13.1, three letters, 2017 and 2018 E.C.).
   - The school applies to the zone each year. The zone's team inspects it against the standard and the staff documents,
     then writes to the regional bureau recommending that the operating licence be **renewed for the fiscal year**.
   - **Two registers go with the letter:**
     - **training vehicles:** vehicle type, plate code and number, engine and chassis numbers, model, service
       ("training"), seats, gross weight, year made, technical condition, insurance (yes or no);
     - **trainers:** name, age, education level, employment (permanent or contract), years of service, agreement, salary,
       own licence grade and instructor qualification.
   - The bureau has its own printed form for the training-vehicle register (H).
   - → **Task 1.7:** the school's yearly renewal is a zone recommendation to the region (a letter with both registers),
     not a zone decision.
2. **Oversight visits end in a findings report** (Q13.2): per school, numbered **strengths** and **weaknesses**.
   - Typical weaknesses: a training vehicle without its yearly bolo or its electrical system working, the school's
     licence not renewed, trainers' licences not renewed, a computer room not working, trainees trained on vehicles not
     assigned to the school.
   - → **Task 1.7:** the oversight visit records findings per school (strength or weakness, free text, optionally
     linked to a vehicle or trainer), which feed the sanctions ladder.
3. **The exam day runs on lists** (Q13.3, Q13.4, annexes 4, 5, 6).
   - The school writes to the zone: these trainees, per grade and **training round** (ዙር), passed the **theory exam**;
     please give them the practical exam. The list: name, grade, round, theory score.
   - **The candidate's appointment card** (the regional bureau's printed card, serial-numbered): photo, school, the
     learner's permit number, the grade trained, the training hours, the theory-test code; a table of appointments
     (theory or practical, attempt, date, time, score %, the examiner).
   - **The practical exam checklist** (the regional bureau's form, 100 points):
     1. pre-drive checks ("tell me and show me" questions, seat and mirrors);
     2. starting the engine;
     3. moving off;
     4. **a 5 km drive** on mixed roads, 22 scored items (signals, speed, lane, observation, U-turn, roundabout,
        emergency stop);
     5. **the simulator**, 4 scored items.
   - **The practical exam register** per school and day: trainee, sex, grade, round, simulator score, practical score,
     total, passed or failed; the technician and **three observers** sign. A motorcycle variant exists.
   - → **Task 1.7:** the practical checklist is a `Checklist` form (task 1.13a) with weighted items; results carry the
     round; the exam register is the session's result sheet with the examiner and observers.
4. **The region prints the licence cards; the zone asks for them in batches** (annex 8, two letters).
   - After the exams, the zone writes to the regional bureau's licensing work process: this many candidates per grade
     passed (for example 3 motorcycle, 6 taxi-1, 9 automobile, 30 public-1); **please print their licences**.
   - **The school has paid the region's 50% share** of the fee for each trainee into the regional bureau's bank
     account, and the deposit slips are attached.
   - **The data list** sent with the letter, one row per candidate: previous card serial (or "NEW"), a 4-digit grade
     code, name, sex, date of birth, the **photograph number** (the grade code and a capture number), zone code, woreda,
     kebele and the school's code. The grade codes read 1111, 1212, 1414 and 1515 for motorcycle, taxi-1, automobile and
     public-1 (**M**: inferred from the counts per grade).
   - → **Task 1.8:** a print batch is a letter to the region with this list and the deposit references; the fee split
     (region 50%) is a fee-rule fact, not a payment ITRMS takes. → **Task 1.12a:** licence card blanks are held where the
     cards are printed (the region); an office records the card serial that comes back.
5. **A licence has a number and a separate card serial** (annexes 11, 12, verification letters).
   - Licence numbers read as **two digits, a hyphen and five digits**, the first two being the region (as in the written
     answers, ADR-0051). An old booklet licence carries a one-digit grade prefix instead.
   - The card (or booklet) carries its own **serial**, printed with its own prefix and six digits. Letters quote both.
   - → **ADR-0051 stands.** **Task 1.6:** the licence keeps the card serial as well as the number.
6. **Renewal needs a medical certificate and a print-fund deposit** (annexes 2, 3).
   - **The zone's notice of requirements:** a deposit of **1,000 Birr** into the regional bureau's print-fund bank
     account, a copy of the licence, the blood group and the school attended; an old paper licence also needs a photo, the
     education level, a birth certificate and (unreadable, L) one more document.
   - **A referral letter** from the zone to a health centre or hospital asks for the holder's medical examination.
   - **The regional health bureau's "Drivers physical examination report"** (Amharic and English): vision (each eye,
     colour: red, amber, green), distance, hearing, other findings, epilepsy, diabetes, hypertension, known heart disease
     (each with or without follow-up), laboratory results, and the recommendation: **grant**, **grant with restrictions or
     conditions**, or **refuse**; the medical officer signs.
   - → **Task 1.6 (and ADR-0052):** the health certificate records the facility, the date and the recommendation
     (with its conditions); the findings stay on the paper (health data is kept to the minimum).
7. **While the region prints, the zone gives a TEMPORARY DRIVING PERMIT LETTER** (annexes 11, 12).
   - "To whom it may concern": the holder of licence number … (card serial …), whose renewal is due (or whose licence
     was lost, with a police report), may drive **between two dates** until the regional bureau prints the licence.
     The windows seen are about 3 and 9 months.
   - → **Task 1.6:** like the temporary bolo (task 1.3), a renewal or replacement can end in a temporary permit (QR, a
     validity window), closed when the printed card is handed over.
8. **Licence verification letters** (two, Amharic and English): at the holder's request, the zone confirms that the
   licence of a given grade, number and card serial, issued and expiring on given dates, "has equal value to the federal
   licence of that grade". → **Task 1.6:** the verification service issues this letter in either language.
9. **Licence files move between regions by letter** (annex, incoming): the zone asks another region's licensing
   authority to send the original file of a driver who now lives in the zone (the old grades and numbers quoted).
   → **Task 1.6:** the licence transfer-in records the sending authority and the file received.
10. **Money is collected on the regional finance bureau's receipt vouchers** (annex 7, two vouchers).
    - The **Receipt Voucher** (form መ/ሂ/7/ለ and መ/ሂ/7/ሐ, ME/HE) is printed for the finance bureau in **lettered series**
      ("ሴሪ/ለ/12ኛ", "ሴሪ/ሐ/4ኛ": an Ethiopic letter and a printing) with a **six-digit serial**, in pads with three copies
      (the payer, accounts, the pad).
    - It records the public body, the payer and address, the **mode of collection** (cash, cheque, deposit slip, bank
      transfer, with a cheque or reference number), the amount in figures and words, the purpose, and for accounts the
      **budget category and account code**, debit and credit.
    - → **Task 1.12a (done in this task):** receipt leaves are received from the **finance bureau** as a source, in an
      **Ethiopic series**. → **Task 1.12:** the pad receipt entry takes the same series (the payment's pad-series field is
      Latin-only today), and the cashier's remittance lists the account code.
11. **The old regional libre booklet** (annex): vehicle description, the plates (office code and code-number), owner,
    possessor, a declaration under a **5-Birr revenue stamp**, and **a renewals table**: date, annual sticker (bolo)
    number, the registration officer and the **receipt number**, one row per year.
    → **Task 1.3:** a bolo records the sticker serial and the receipt number together, as the booklet did.
12. **Already recorded** and seen again: the 25-service application form (twice), the vehicle sale contract, the vehicle
    data transfer document (here with a plate history across four regions), the temporary libre and the libre data page,
    an insurer's sticker, a regional 2018 bolo sticker (public enterprise; its **counterfoil carries the same serial**).
13. **Annex A of the questionnaire, ticked:** the documents the zone says it has include the bolo checklist, the
    temporary bolo letter, the training-school inspection form, the monthly deployment schedule, the association's
    monthly report and renewal forms, and the vehicle capability letter.

## Requests back to the zone (added to R1–R8)

- **R9.** The 4-digit grade codes for every grade (1111 … as read), and whether the photograph number comes from the
  region's capture station.
- **R10.** The fee split for a new licence and a renewal: the region's 50% share, the zone's share, and what the
  1,000-Birr print-fund deposit covers.
- **R11.** Whether a receipt voucher's serial restarts with each printing of a series (ለ 12th, ሐ 4th), or is unique
  within the letter.
- **R12.** Who signs the temporary driving permit, and its longest validity.
