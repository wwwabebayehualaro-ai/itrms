# Security policy

ITRMS is proprietary software of **ABANEXUS SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT PLC** (ABA Nexus Technologies). The rules the product
is built to are in [docs/platform/security.md](docs/platform/security.md).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities **privately**:

* e-mail **abanexustech@gmail.com** with the subject `SECURITY: <short title>`;
* describe the issue, the affected version or installation, and the steps to reproduce it;
* do not include real personal data. If you saw some, say so, and don't copy it.

Do not open a public issue, post the details, or test against a production installation without the bureau's written
permission.

## What happens next

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| We confirm we received the report | 2 working days |
| First assessment (severity, affected installations) | 5 working days |
| Fix or mitigation available | critical 7 days (mitigation within 72 hours), high 14 days, medium 30 days, low 90 days |
| Affected bureaus told | within 24 hours of confirming that an installation is affected |

Bureaus report incidents to Ethio-CERT and personal-data breaches to the data protection authority as the law requires
(Proclamation 1426/2026, PDP Proclamation 1321/2024). We support them with the facts and the evidence.

## Supported versions

Security fixes go to the current release and the previous one (N and N−1). Installations on older releases are upgraded
as part of the fix.

## Good-faith research

We will not pursue anyone who reports in good faith, follows this policy, avoids privacy violations and service
disruption, and gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before telling anyone else.
